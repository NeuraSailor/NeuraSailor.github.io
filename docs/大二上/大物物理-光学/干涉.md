### 干涉位置

$\delta=nr_{2}-nr_{1}=nd\cdot\theta=nd\cdot \frac{x}{D}$ = $\begin{cases} \pm k\lambda_{0}, & k=0,1,2… \text{明纹} \\ \pm(2k-1)\frac{\lambda_{0}}{2}, & k=1,2,3… \text{暗纹} \end{cases}$

### 光强分布

$A^2=A_1^2+A_2^2+2A_1A_2\cos\Delta\varphi$

$\implies I=I_1+I_2+2\sqrt{I_1I_2}\cos\Delta\varphi$

若 $I_{1}=I_{2}$, $\implies I=2I_1(1+\cos\Delta\varphi)=4I_1\cos^2\frac{\Delta\varphi}{2}$

### 白光重叠

$x=\frac{(k+1)\lambda D}{d}=\frac{k(\lambda+\Delta\lambda)D}{d}$

当 $k\frac{\Delta\lambda}\lambda=1$ 即 $k=\frac\lambda{\Delta\lambda}$ 或 $\Delta\lambda=\frac\lambda\kappa$ 发生重级
